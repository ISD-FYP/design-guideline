Typography

Fraunces + Avenir Next

The serif speaks — hero, titles, card heads, numbers. The sans works — body, labels, captions. Deck fonts are Canva fonts; PowerPoint fallbacks are Cambria Bold / Calibri. Sizes below show the deck's native pt (at 20×11.25in) and the PowerPoint equivalent (÷1.5).

Do & don't

guardrails

Do

  • one loud terracotta moment per slide beyond the eyebrow
  • sharp corners, 0.75pt hairline borders, flat fills
  • let Fraunces carry titles, card heads, every number
  • emphasize with a colored border or apricot-tint fill
  • eyebrow carries the midterm's section names

Don't

  • rounded corners, pills, chips — none exist in either deck
  • shadows, gradients, glassmorphism
  • a rule under content-slide titles (only the cover dash)
  • photos, or art on every page
  • sage text below 15pt; sage as background
  • pure white canvas, pure black ink
